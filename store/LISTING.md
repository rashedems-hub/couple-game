### Full description
```
Halalii is a light, fun game for married couples that brings you closer and breaks the routine with laughs and sweet words.

💞 4 games in one app
🎴 Cards: draw a card and do the dare, and your partner reacts and gives you points. Three levels: Light, Warm and Spicy.
💭 Know Me? One of you answers about yourself in secret, and the other guesses.
🤔 Would You Rather: you both pick in secret, then the choices are revealed. Same pick? Points for both of you!
🎡 Lucky Wheel: spin the wheel and see which dare it picks for you.

📱 Play your way
• Each on your own phone: create a room and send the link to your partner. The game runs on both phones together.
• Or on one phone: pass it between you, with a "pass the phone" screen before secret answers.

✨ Features
• Every dare and question is worded for the husband or the wife, depending on whose turn it is.
• Points with a weekly and an all-time leaderboard, and you can hide yourself from it.
• A "Quran & Sunnah" page with authentic verses and hadiths on love and kindness between spouses.
• A "Relationship health" page with general medical information, written respectfully.
• Arabic and English, and each partner can play in their own language in the same room.
• Several color themes and gentle sounds.
• Play as a guest without signing up, and delete your account and data at any time.
• No ads.

Halalii is for married couples aged 18 and over. Every dare is optional and can be skipped at any time.

Contact & feedback: halalii.app@gmail.com
```

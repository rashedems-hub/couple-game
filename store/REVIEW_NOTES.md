# Notes for App Review (Apple / Google Play)

Paste the English text below into **App Review Information → Notes** (App Store Connect)
or **App access** (Play Console). No sign-in is needed.

---

Halalii is a game for married couples. Two players normally use two phones, but the app
also has a one-phone mode so you can review everything on a single device:

1. Open the app. If a welcome screen appears, tap "Try as a guest" (no account needed).
2. Tap "Start a new game".
3. Enter any name, pick Husband or Wife, then under "How will you play?" choose "One phone"
   and enter a partner name.
4. Tap "Create room". The game starts right away and the turn passes between the two
   players on the same phone.
5. Use the four tabs at the top to try each game: Cards, Know Me?, Would You Rather and
   Lucky Wheel. In Know Me? and Would You Rather, a "Pass the phone" screen appears so the
   second player's answer stays secret; tap the button to continue.

Other notes:
- Account creation is optional (email and password). Accounts can be deleted in the app:
  Terms & Conditions → "Delete my account and data".
- Privacy policy: https://rashedems-hub.github.io/couple-game/privacy.html
- The app contains no ads and no purchases.
- The app is intended for married adults (18+). Content is romantic but not explicit.
- The language follows the device (Arabic or English) and can be switched with the
  EN / عربي button on the home screen.
